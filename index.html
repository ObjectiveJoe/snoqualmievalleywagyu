Premium Grass-Fed & Finished Wagyu Beef from Broad Acres Farm

Raised by Joseph and Diana Hayes in the fertile Snoqualmie Valley

Snoqualmie Valley Wagyu is raised on the wide fertile valley grasses of the Snoqualmie Valley.

At Snoqualmie Valley Wagyu, we specialize in raising 100% grass-fed and grass-finished Wagyu beef on our organically managed farm. We believe in:

  • Small, closed-herd operation - Every cow is born and raised here at Broad Acres Farm
  • Personal care - We personally tend to each animal daily, ensuring their health and wellbeing
  • Humane practices - From birth through processing with our partners Del Fox Meats
  • Quality over quantity - We raise a limited number of cattle each year to maintain exceptional standards
