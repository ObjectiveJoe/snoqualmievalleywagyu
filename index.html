What is F3 Wagyu?

You may be wondering what "F3" means when we describe our cattle. F3 stands for third-generation crossbred Wagyu, representing a careful breeding program that combines the best of both worlds:

The Breeding Process:

  • F1 (First Generation): Purebred Wagyu bull × American Angus cow = 50% Wagyu genetics
  • F2 (Second Generation): F1 cow bred back to Wagyu bull = 75% Wagyu genetics
  • F3 (Third Generation): F2 cow bred back to Wagyu bull = 87.5% Wagyu genetics

Why F3 is ideal for grass-finishing:

  • Superior marbling - F3 cattle retain the exceptional marbling (intramuscular fat) that makes Wagyu famous, while being hardy enough to thrive on 100% grass
  • Grass-fed adaptation - The American Angus genetics provide excellent foraging ability and the robust constitution needed for our Pacific Northwest climate
  • Flavor profile - Grass-finishing produces a more complex, nuanced beef flavor compared to grain-finishing, with the tenderness and marbling Wagyu is known for
  • Health benefits - Grass-fed beef is higher in omega-3 fatty acids, CLA (conjugated linoleic acid), and vitamins A and E

Purebred vs. Crossbred Wagyu: While 100% purebred Wagyu (like those raised in Japan) produces the most extreme marbling, they traditionally require grain-finishing to achieve that result. Our F3 crossbreeding program allows us to raise cattle that develop exceptional marbling on grass alone - giving you the health benefits of grass-fed beef with the eating experience of premium Wagyu.

Our F3 Wagyu represents years of careful genetic selection, combining the legendary marbling and tenderness of Japanese Wagyu with cattle that thrive on the lush grasses of the Snoqualmie Valley.

Our Sustainable, Low-Carbon Approach

At Broad Acres Farm, we're committed to raising beef in harmony with the environment. Our operation maintains an exceptionally low carbon footprint through regenerative farming practices:

  • 100% local, natural grasses - Our cattle graze on the naturally occurring grasses of the Snoqualmie Valley year-round, requiring no imported feed
  • Estate-grown winter hay - We harvest our own hay from these same pastures, eliminating the need to transport feed from distant sources
  • Closed-loop system - From birth to butchering, our cattle never leave the valley, minimizing transportation emissions
  • Regenerative grazing - Our grass-based system naturally sequesters carbon in the soil while building soil health
  • No grain finishing - We avoid the carbon-intensive grain production, transportation, and processing required for conventional feedlot operations
  • Organic management - Our pastures are managed without synthetic fertilizers or pesticides, further reducing our environmental impact

The result? Beef raised with one of the smallest carbon footprints possible - nourished entirely by sunshine, rain, and the rich valley soil, with minimal inputs and virtually no food miles. When you choose Snoqualmie Valley Wagyu, you're supporting a truly sustainable, local food system.
